## Account

**How do I create an account?**
Go to `/auth?mode=signup`. Enter your name, email, password, phone number, and preferred language, then confirm your email if prompted.

**How do I reset my password?**
On the login page, select "Forgot password" and follow the email link sent to you. If you don't receive it, contact support@premiereservices.ca.

**How do I change my email language (EN/FR)?**
Go to Dashboard → My Account → set your preferred language for emails and AI support → Save.

**Where is my dashboard?**
Click "My Dashboard" in the main menu, or go directly to `/dashboard`.

**How do I update my postal code or address?**
Go to Dashboard → My Account → update your postal code/address → Save. This keeps your nearby-pro matching and receipts accurate.

## Booking

**How do I book a pro near me?**
Enter your postal code, browse or search `/services` by category, open a pro's profile, and use the booking wizard to pick a service, date/time, and location.

**Why do you need my postal code?**
It's used to match you with pros in your area and to calculate distance for search results and receipts.

**What happens after I send a request?**
The pro can accept, decline, or send you a quote. You'll see updates in your Dashboard, plus email/SMS if enabled. If they send a quote, you can accept or decline it from your Dashboard.

**Can I cancel or reschedule a booking?**
Each service (or the pro’s default policy) uses one of three cancellation rules. **You must read and accept the policy shown in a highlighted box before you can continue booking.** The options are:

1. **Free cancellation** — cancel without a cancellation fee under that policy.  
2. **Late fee** — if you cancel **less than 24 hours** before the service, a fee applies. It may be a **fixed dollar amount** (e.g. $20 on a $60 service → about $40 refunded if already paid) or **25% / 50% / 75%** of the service price. Cancelling earlier than 24 hours may avoid that late fee.  
3. **No cancellation / full charge** — once confirmed, the booking cannot be cancelled without being charged **100%** of the service price.

The chosen policy is snapshotted on your booking when you request it. Partial refunds of an already-paid Square payment are processed through Square Refunds. For help with an existing booking, contact support@premiereservices.ca.

**Why was my booking declined?**
The pro may be unavailable, outside your area for that date, or unable to take the job for another reason. You can try another pro nearby or reach out to support@premiereservices.ca.

## ID Verification

**Why do you ask for ID?**
We collect ID to verify identity for booking safety — both clients and pros go through this. For clients, only the front of your ID is required.

**Is my ID public?**
No. Your ID is private and only viewable by the pro assigned to your booking, for identity verification purposes. It never appears on any public profile.

**How do I replace my ID photo?**
Go to Dashboard → My Account → open the yellow shield (identity verification) → upload a new photo → save/update.

## Payments

**How do I pay?**
Payment is processed securely through Square, typically after the pro accepts your booking or quote.

**What fees do clients pay?**
Using the marketplace to browse and book is free. You pay the service price plus any taxes/fees shown at checkout — no separate client platform fee.

**What is the 5% fee for pros?**
Première Services charges a 5% platform fee on completed transactions paid through the platform.

**When do I get a refund?**
Refunds are handled case-by-case and are not automatically guaranteed. Contact support@premiereservices.ca with your booking details to request one. Cancellation fees shown at booking may still apply under the pro’s policy.

## Pros / Plans

**How do I become a pro?**
Create an account, go to `/join-pros`, complete your business profile (services, area, photos, ID/selfie, tax numbers), and choose a plan. You can explore the pro dashboard while setting up. **Your services are not advertised in client search and are not bookable until you have an active paid plan (Starter, Growth, or Pro)** and admin verification as required.

**What's the difference between Starter, Growth, and Pro?**
- **Starter (CA$20/mo):** good for testing the platform, ~20 requests.
- **Growth (CA$27/mo):** stronger profile, scheduling/calendar extras, bundles and auto-reply, ~50 requests.
- **Pro (CA$32/mo):** everything in Growth plus SMS automation and the booking AI assistant, no listed request cap.

**What is the request limit on Starter/Growth?**
Starter includes roughly 20 requests per month; Growth includes roughly 50.

**Do I need a subscription to appear in search?**
**Yes.** Only pros with an **active paid plan** (Starter, Growth, or Pro) appear in client search results and can take bookings through the marketplace listing. Unpaid / hold accounts cannot advertise services to clients.

**How do I set my cancellation policy?**
Two places: (1) **Dashboard → My Services → Add/Edit service** — set free, fixed $ late fee, % late fee, or no cancel for that service. (2) **Dashboard → My Account → Cancellation policy** — default for bookings. Clients must accept the policy shown at booking. For a fixed fee example: $60 massage + $20 cancel if under 24h → keep $20 / refund ~$40 after Square payment via a partial refund.

## Safety / Support

**Are pros verified?**
Yes, pros go through admin verification (ID and, where applicable, licence review) before full listing. Paid plan is also required to appear in search.

**Who do I contact if something goes wrong?**
Reach out to support@premiereservices.ca, or use the Help button / `/support` for AI-assisted help. For a specific booking issue, you can also file a claim from your Dashboard.

**Is Première available in my city?**
Coverage is expanding city by city — enter your postal code on the site to check availability in your area.

## Off-Topic Handling

The Support AI only answers questions about Première Services (accounts, bookings, plans, payments, ID, how the platform works). It must refuse anything else — homework help, shopping recommendations, weather, recipes, general medical/legal advice, etc. — and redirect the user back to Première-related topics or support@premiereservices.ca.

**Example refusals:**
- "Can you write my essay?" → "I can only help with questions about Première Services — accounts, bookings, payments, and plans. For anything else, I'm not able to help."
- "What's the weather today?" → same redirect.
- "Should I buy new skis?" → same redirect.
- "Give me a recipe" → same redirect.

Keep refusals short, friendly, and always end by pointing back to what the AI *can* help with, or to support@premiereservices.ca for anything outside its scope.
