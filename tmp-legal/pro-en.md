3. Identity verification, licences, and “verified” claims
3.1 Identity. You consent to Company collecting and using your government photo ID and selfie to verify your identity for Platform safety.
3.2 Biometric / selfie. If the selfie (or ID image) is processed by a method that captures biometric characteristics or measurements for identity verification, Quebec’s Act to establish a legal framework for information technology, CQLR c C-1.1, s. 44, requires prior disclosure to the Commission d’accès à l’information (CAI) and the express consent of the person concerned, and only the minimum characteristics needed. Creating a biometric database requires disclosure to the CAI not later than 60 days before it is brought into service (s. 45). A non-biometric alternative is offered where required.
3.3 Licences. For categories that require a Quebec licence, permit, or professional-order membership, you must supply a current number and keep it accurate. Company may check public sources.
3.4 What “verified” means. Any Platform badge or label such as “licence checked,” “ID reviewed,” or similar means only that, as of the stated date, Company performed the limited check described on the Platform (for example: RBQ open-data match and/or manual lookup of a public register, plus admin review of ID). It does not mean: continuous monitoring; insurance confirmation; skills testing; criminal background clearance; or that the Pro is an employee or agent of Company.
3.5 Misrepresentation. You must not claim a licence you do not hold. Company must not advertise a Pro as “fully verified” or “government certified” if only a partial public lookup was done.

Licence-check practice:
RBQ (construction / related trade licences): may be auto-checked against RBQ open data (JSON/CSV of active licences). Plumber / electrician style certificates typically map to RBQ licence categories.
Other Quebec registers (manual public lookup unless an API is available): BSP public register; MAPAQ permit list; MELCCFP pesticide registries; Ordre des CPA; Ordre des psychologues; SAAQ recognized driving-school lists. Status date is marked on the Pro profile.
